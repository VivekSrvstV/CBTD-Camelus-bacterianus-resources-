# CBTD-Camelus-bacterianus-resources-
Tissue-Specific Transcriptome Analysis in Camelus bacterianus 
Background: Camelus bacterianus belongs to Camelidae, a mammal with long legs, a big-lipped snout and a humped back. In the current study, the Transcriptome of Camelus bacterianus was sequenced by a Next-generation sequencing (NGS) technologies (Illumina’s Genome Analyzer IIx system). The transcriptome of Camelus bacterianus has been annotated and submitted to NCBI Bio-Project with Accession No. PRJNA62509.
Results: The sequencing of Camelus bacterianus transcriptome by Illumina’s Genome Analyzer IIx system of Nine tissue-sample (Brain, Hypothalamus, Liver, Heart, Lungs, Kidney, Muscle, Skin and Testis), yielded 309 million paired-end reads with average length of 76 bp.  The de-novo assembly produce 3,07,343 transcripts with average length of 460 bp using Trinity assembler. We identified more than 4,240 simple sequence repeats. Total number of transcripts hits with NCBI database are 23849, the function and Gene ontology terms were assigned to 55917, were annotated with biological process 18166, Cellular component with 19313 and Molecular functions with 18438 assigned, respectively. The transcripts annotated for Enzyme Commission number are 2288 and various domains 15771, reported. We also identified tissue-specific gene in different tissue in Camelus bacterianus, 498 in brain, 80 in Heart, 1 in Hypothalamus, 19 in kidney, 63 in Liver, 199 in Lungs, 18 in Muscle, 167 in Skin and 539 in Testis. Finally, we have developed an Interface in a Public domain, Camelus bacterianus Transcriptome Database (CBTD), which implement, public access to the data and results reported in this study.  
Conclusions: Next-Generation Sequencing technologies provide new insights into the genome research of Camelus bacterianus. We reported here was to generate a high-quality transcriptome sequences, assemblies and annotation for Camelus bacterianus. The study provides a highly assembled and accurate transcripts with Gene ontology (GO), Enzyme Commission number and various Domain. The Transcriptome database (CBTD) developed will be highly useful to the research community in accessing transcriptome information of the Camelus bacterianus.   

Key words:  Camelus Bactrianus, Next-generation sequencing (NGS)
